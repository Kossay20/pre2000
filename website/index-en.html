Your pregnancy companion

Everything you need, in one app.

Key Features

Designed with love to support every step of your journey.

📅

Appointments and reminders

Scans, blood work, check-ups: your appointments in one place, with an account. Reminders for vitamins, hydration, walking and sleep, set to your own rhythm.

⚖️

Weight Tracking

Log your weight and follow it on a chart, next to the weight-gain range usually recommended for your week.

🥗

Foods to avoid

What to skip during pregnancy, with the level of risk and the reason why.

📖

Articles

Dozens of articles sorted by topic: nutrition, symptoms, medical exams, birth preparation, postpartum. They inform; they do not replace your caregiver's advice.

💬

Information assistant

Ask your questions in your own language: the assistant points you to the articles that cover them. On a warning sign, it directs you to emergency care and shows your country's emergency number (or 112). It does not give medical advice.

🌍

Multilingual

Available in French, English, Arabic and Tunisian. In Arabic and Tunisian the whole interface runs right to left.
