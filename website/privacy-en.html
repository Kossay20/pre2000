Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

The "Mama & Bébé" app is published by KSA MEDIA LLC. This policy explains what data the app collects, where it is kept, who processes it for us, how long it is kept and how to erase it.

2. Data Collected

Depending on what you use, the app processes:

  • Account: your email address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication to sign you in.
  • Pregnancy profile: first name, last name (optional), country, city, age range, whether this is your first pregnancy, last period date, due date, height and pre-pregnancy weight, cycle length if you track fertility, and your chosen language. You enter these yourself.
  • Health data you record: weight, blood pressure, blood glucose, symptoms, baby movements, contractions, medical appointments, reminders and tasks, your hospital bag, with any notes you add. You decide what to record; the app infers no diagnosis from it.
  • Declared medical follow-up: if you choose to tell us, whether a healthcare professional has flagged gestational diabetes, pregnancy hypertension or a twin pregnancy for you. The question is optional and you can change or turn it off at any time in Settings. It is used only to adjust which reminders are offered to you.
  • Emergency contacts: the names and numbers of the people you add yourself.
  • If you have an account, this data is stored on Google Firebase, encrypted in transit, and access rules restrict it to you alone.
  • Kept only on your phone: hydration, Ramadan tracking, daily checklist and saved articles. They are not sent to our servers.
  • Assistant: it runs entirely on your phone. Your messages are neither sent to a server nor saved.
  • Technical data: when an error occurs, a technical report (error description, screen, device model and operating system, app version) with a pseudonymous identifier derived from your account, never your email address (via Sentry).

3. Use of Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide pregnancy tracking and schedule your reminders on your phone.
  • Sync your data across your devices (if you are signed in).
  • Fix app errors using technical reports (Sentry).

We do not sell, rent or share any personal data with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes, and we do no profiling.

Legal basis: we process your pregnancy and health data with your explicit agreement: before saving your profile, the app asks you to tick a box allowing it to use this data to provide its features. Without that agreement, your profile is not saved. The date of your agreement and the version of this policy are kept with your profile.

4. Service Providers

These services process data on our behalf, only to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud): sign-in, database, and checking that requests come from the genuine app (App Check, using Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity). Firebase Policy.
  • Sentry: receives the error reports described in section 2. Sentry Policy.
  • Expo: delivers app updates; it receives technical device information (IP address, app version) when the app checks for an update. Expo Policy.
  • Google Fonts: loads fonts when you generate your PDF journal; Google then receives your device's IP address.

5. Storage, Security and Transfers

All communications between the app and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Your account data is stored on Google Cloud Platform's infrastructure (Firebase). The publisher is based in the United States, and Google and our providers may process your data outside your country, including in the United States.

6. Retention

Your account data is kept as long as your account exists and erased as soon as you delete it; we keep no copy. Data kept on your phone stays there until you erase it, delete your account or uninstall the app. Error reports are deleted automatically by Sentry at the end of its retention period.

Data you save on our servers as a guest, under an anonymous identifier (section 8), stays there until you erase it with Settings → My Data → Erase my data; if you create an account, it becomes your account's data. We do not delete it automatically today: if you uninstall the app without erasing it first, it stays on our servers under that anonymous identifier, with no email address, and the app can no longer reach it.

7. Your Rights

The GDPR grants you these rights, and the app implements them without going through us:

  • Access and portability (art. 15 and 20): export your data as JSON from Settings → My Data → Export. The export contains your profile and health data, including your declared medical follow-up.
  • Erasure (art. 17): My Profile → Danger zone → Delete my account, or Settings → My Data → Delete my account. Immediate and permanent: account, profile, health data, appointments, reminders, emergency contacts, and the local copy on your device. See Delete my account.
  • Rectification (art. 16): anything you entered can be edited in the app.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw the agreement given when you created your profile at any time by erasing your data: Settings → My Data → Delete my account with an account, or Settings → My Data → Erase my data without one. The declared medical follow-up can be turned off separately in Settings → Medical follow-up, without deleting the rest of your account.
  • By email: contact@mama-et-bebe.com for any request regarding your data.

8. Guest Mode

Without an account, you give no email address. What you record is kept on your phone. Some of it (for example health entries, appointments and reminders) may also be saved on our servers under an anonymous identifier, with no email address, so that the app works; apart from that, only technical error reports (section 2) leave your phone. Baby movements, contractions and your hospital bag stay only on your phone until you create an account; they are then saved to it. To erase everything, on your phone and on our servers: Settings → My Data → Erase my data. Uninstalling the app erases only what is on your phone.
