Delete your account and your data

You do not need to write to us or wait for a reply. Deletion happens inside the app, takes effect immediately, and is permanent.

From the app

  1. Open Mama & Bébé and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the icon at the top right of your profile).
  3. Data & Privacy → Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. That's it.

What is erased

  • Your account and your email address.
  • Your pregnancy profile: last period date, due date, first name, country, age range, height, pre-pregnancy weight.
  • Your declared medical follow-up, if you provided one.
  • All of your health data: weight, blood pressure, glucose, symptoms, baby movements, contractions.
  • Your appointments, reminders, tasks and hospital-bag checklist.
  • Your assistant conversations and saved articles.
  • Your emergency contacts.
  • The local copy of all of it on your phone.

Nothing is kept and nothing is archived. There is no grace period during which your data would remain recoverable: deletion is final the moment you confirm it. If you want a copy, export your data first — the button is in the same Settings section.

If you no longer have access to the app

Lost phone, app uninstalled, forgotten password: email contact@mama-et-bebe.com from your account's email address, with "Account deletion" as the subject. We handle the request within 30 days, as the GDPR provides, and confirm by email when it is done.

If you use the app without an account

In guest mode your data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app erases it. You can also use Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete my account, which clears local storage without uninstalling anything.
