Delete your account and your data

You do not need to write to us or wait for a reply. Deletion happens inside the app, takes effect immediately, and is permanent.

From the app

  1. Open Mama & Bébé and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, tap the circle with your initial at the top right: the My Profile screen opens.
  3. Scroll down to Danger zone → Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. If you have not signed in recently, the app asks for your password again before erasing anything. That's it.

Other path, same deletion: My Profile → Notification Settings → Settings screen, My Data section → Delete my account.

If your internet connection drops during deletion, the app tells you and keeps your account so you can run the deletion again: it never deletes the account while leaving data behind it.

What is erased

  • Your account and your email address.
  • Your pregnancy profile: last period date, due date, first and last name, country, age range, height, pre-pregnancy weight.
  • Your declared medical follow-up, if you provided one.
  • All of your health data: weight, blood pressure, glucose, symptoms, baby movements, contractions, your hospital bag, and the notes attached to them.
  • Your appointments, reminders and tasks.
  • Your emergency contacts.
  • Everything kept on your phone: hydration, daily checklist, saved articles, and the local copy of all of the above.

The assistant runs on your phone and does not save your conversations, so there is nothing to erase there.

Nothing is kept and nothing is archived. There is no grace period during which your data would remain recoverable: deletion is final the moment you confirm it. If you want a copy, export your data first: Settings → My Data → Export my data.

If you no longer have access to the app

Lost phone, app uninstalled, forgotten password: email contact@mama-et-bebe.com from your account's email address, with "Account deletion" as the subject. We handle the request within 30 days, as the GDPR provides, and confirm by email when it is done.

If you use the app without an account

In guest mode what you record is kept on your phone, and some of it may also be saved on our servers under an anonymous identifier, with no email address. Use Settings → My Data → Erase my data (or My Profile → Danger zone → Reset Profile): it erases your data on your phone and on our servers. Uninstalling the app erases only what is on your phone.
